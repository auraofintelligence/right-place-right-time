Payment rails / inherited from the stall

Fast lane or fee-light lane — your call.

Automated card or mobile-wallet checkout is the quickest path. PayID, bank transfer and cash help avoid payment-processor fees, but they are checked and fulfilled manually most days, not constantly — delivery waits until payment is confirmed. Direct and crypto rails are on the bench for later: only ever send funds to a directly verified address, and placeholder text on a draft page is never a live payment address.

Money, tax and fee specifics get verified live before anything is charged — nothing on this page is financial or legal advice. The working terms, ABN and current rails live at Strange but True, the reference implementation.

The public record

The Honour Board.

One board across all four tiers: who supported what, what work was paid for, and which idea moved forward — with the tier it touched. Named entries are opt-in; quiet support is equally honoured.
