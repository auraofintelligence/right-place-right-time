Tier 03 / Regional — Oceania

The thinnest shelf, on purpose.

Oceania is not underdeveloped here — it is undemanded. The pattern is offered across the Blue Pacific as a fork under local names and local laws, never an export. This shelf fills when invitations arrive, not before. That restraint is the feature.

Audience, provisionally: Pacific kin, regional builders, festival and residency hosts

The invitation

Host a stop. Shape the pattern.

The most useful support at this tier is often not money: an invitation to a festival, a residency, a community wanting its own fork of the civic pattern or its own place-twin. A hosted stop puts the work in the right place at the right time — which is the whole mission. Sponsoring a leg of travel does the same in currency.
