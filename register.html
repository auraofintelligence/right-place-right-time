The register / the plain-text truth

Everything I build, on one page.

Sixteen projects, four honest lenses: where each one lives, how real it is today, whether it travels, and whether it's proven record or still becoming. No JavaScript needed — this is the plain table the prettier pages are drawn from, and I re-shelve it as the work moves.

The sorting logic

Four axes, confirm or replace.

Each project answers four questions. The axes themselves are up for revision on the seams page.

01

Scale

Local, Australia, Oceania or world — where the project inherently lives. One home shelf each, even when the pattern travels.

02

Horizon

Proven (live and evidential), active (live and growing) or horizon (described ahead of code, by design — unbuilt is not backlog, it is invitation).

03

Portability

In place (earns and functions only where it stands, like a market stall) or travels (music, writing, remote systems work). Portability matters more as scale widens.

04

Register

Record (gold, solid — established and citable) or proposal (purple, dashed — my own provisional frame: react, revise, refuse).

What's not on this table, and why

The workshop goes deeper than this page.

Behind these sixteen sit around 110 public repos — experiments, sketches, labs, half-songs. This table carries the ones strong enough to stand in front of strangers; the rest come off the shelf in later passes. And a few pieces of my public work I'm still deciding whether to show at this particular door — that call lives on the workbench, where I keep all the ones I haven't made yet.

Built in the open — gold is what's real today, purple is what's still becoming. Back something here and something real comes back.
